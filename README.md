# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository features an interactive **Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard** built using **Power BI**. The project focuses on converting operational transactional data from a coffee shop retail business into actionable commercial insights. It enables store managers and business stakeholders to analyze revenue generation, product performance, and peak foot traffic hours to optimize inventory and staffing.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Key Metrics & Insights
Based on the data modeling and DAX measures implemented in this dashboard, here are the core business performance insights:

* **💰 Total Revenue Generated:** The dashboard analyzes a total sales volume reaching **$698.81K** across all product categories.
* **📦 Transaction Volume:** A total of **149.12K individual orders** were processed and analyzed to understand consumer habits.
* **☕ Top Product Categories:** **Coffee** leads as the primary revenue driver, generating **$269.95K**, closely followed by **Tea** which generated **$196.41K**.
* **📈 Highest Performing Product:** The **Brewed Regular Coffee** stands out as the ultimate top-selling individual item in terms of total revenue.
* **⏰ Peak Operating Hours:** Time-series analysis indicates that **8:00 AM to 10:00 AM** represents the peak foot traffic and highest sales density period during the day.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Skills Demonstrated
By developing this project, I applied end-to-end Business Intelligence (BI) methodologies:
* **Data Extraction & ETL:** Used **Power Query** for advanced data cleaning, transforming text-based timestamps into continuous datetime models, and restructuring raw data.
* **Data Modeling:** Established a clean dimensional modeling approach (Star Schema) separating Fact tables (Sales Transactions) from Dimension tables (Products, Stores, and Calendar).
* **Analytical Calculations:** Authored custom **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** measures for metrics such as total revenue, order counts, and hourly sales distributions.
* **Data Storytelling & UI Design:** Designed a cohesive, clean user interface leveraging proper alignment, visual hierarchy, and intuitive color palettes tailored to the food and beverage industry.
